# Contribution and invariant coverage

## What changes a decision

OpenSSH already specifies connection-associated binding and cooperating hops.
Source inspection predicts why the filter cannot support constrained signing.
The experiments add executable intervention, named-product outcomes and the
observed difference between physical transport and agent-visible context. The
deployment decision is whether a particular endpoint supports all required policy
dimensions while retaining the existing credential source. No universal mechanism,
priority, prevalence, field adoption or vulnerability-severity claim is made.

| Obligation/invariant | Historical basis | Current evidence | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Permitted use and destination exclusion | Native gates, stock/repair/revert, 2×2 factorial | Three state-v1 direct/topology fixture schedules; shipping V2-F | Fixed source policies and destinations |
| Identity exclusion at signing | Known-B probes | Both state phases, bound/unbound B; four-source integration | No claim from enumeration alone |
| Accepted binding and signed-data consistency | 23 historical state-case labels | All 23 labels × two phases × native/repaired × three fixtures | Finite cases; native unconstrained signing is not a grammar filter |
| Connection ownership | Historical labels lacked client IDs | Recorded distinct connections, own/other session requests, synchronized live clients | No arbitrary-interleaving proof |
| Binding transitions | Older repeated/reordered-binding records | Current exact frames and accepted/rejected responses | Rejected binding does not erase earlier accepted context |
| Restart | Earlier request sequences | Process event, retained/fresh IDs and fresh real authentication | No automatic retained-path recovery |
| Management restriction | Historical management corpus | Five non-private management/extension requests and A/B health | Addition and complete management semantics untested here |
| Application forwarding context | Historical product observations | V2-F decoded destination-only chain; DevPod matched-topology attempt retained | DevPod fixed-HOP comparison cannot qualify with observed rotating host keys |
| Source-retention rationale | Signing interface and source-selection repair | Source/mediator responsibility model; dedicated-agent baseline | Organizational restriction is an explicit assumption, not field evidence |

The state cases are selected by semantic distinction rather than their count:
identity, signed-data domain, authenticity of supplied context, identity/session/
host consistency, forwarding flags, ordering, isolation and failure lifecycle.
The copied checker-owned specification supplies expected signing behavior; the
new checker validates public bytes and temporal/connection relationships without
importing the live runner.
